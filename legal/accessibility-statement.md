Accessibility Statement

Last updated 15 October 2026

We want Swadeshi Appstore to be usable by as many people as possible. Here is what the app does today, stated plainly.

What we provide
Readable text that follows your device font size. Light, dark and system themes, and a choice of accent colours. Controls are sized for comfortable tapping. Buttons and icons carry labels so screen readers can announce them. The app works fully offline, so a poor connection does not block it.

What we do not claim
We have not carried out a formal accessibility audit, so we do not claim conformance with WCAG or any other standard. We will not state a level we cannot prove.

Telling us about a problem
If something is hard to use, email vasudev.work@rediffmail.com and describe it. We will do our best to fix it.

If you find a security problem in Swadeshi Appstore, please tell us privately first.

How to report
Email vasudev.work@rediffmail.com with a short description, the steps to reproduce it and the app version. Please do not open a public issue before we have had a chance to fix it.

What to expect
We will acknowledge your message and reply within 30 days. If the report is valid we will fix it in an app update and credit you if you wish.

Please do not
Do not access, change or delete other people's data, do not run denial-of-service tests, and do not test on a device you do not own.
